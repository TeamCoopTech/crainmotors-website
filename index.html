About

Two storefronts, side by side, for the everyday things your car needs.

The Crain Motor Cars Service Center is in Suite 24 at 512 Crain Hwy N in Glen Burnie. That's where we handle diagnostics, tune ups, oil changes, brakes and Maryland State Inspections.

Next door in Suite 25 you'll find new and used tires, alignments and Crain Tag & Title.

We're open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and closed on Sunday. Give us a call if you have a question before you stop by.
